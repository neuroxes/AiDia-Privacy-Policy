From download to your first meal analysis in under two minutes

1

Download & Set Up Your Profile

Install AiDia free on iOS or Android. Enter your diabetes type, HbA1c target, insulin information, and daily carb goals in a quick 2-minute setup.

2

Log Your Meal Your Way
